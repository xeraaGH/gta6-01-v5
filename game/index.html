HOW TO PLAY

Walk with WASD, drive with WASD + SHIFT boost. Press E near any car to get in — parked cars are free, moving cars you steal (and the police notice). Wanted stars rise when you steal, speed through the city, shoot, or hit pedestrians. Lose the cops by breaking line of sight; at 4+ stars expect roadblocks and the police helicopter.

Missions: press M to start the next story mission. Yellow markers guide you. Finish missions to earn cash, unlock crew and raise district standing. T starts a taxi fare. Hidden packages glow gold — find all 20.

Switch leads with C. Dante drives faster; Mara shakes the police faster. Your crew — Fixer, Hacker, Lookout — unlocks as you finish missions. Radio: R cycles ALBA FM / NEON 88 / SURF 102.
